A NOTE FROM WURZELRAUM

What would actually change

If you move to Friedenau, the biggest change is the pace of your days. Kita drop-off becomes a walk under plane trees instead of along Torstraße, and the Volkspark is close enough for after-school picnics.

Rents are noticeably lower, which likely turns that third room from a stretch into a given. The honest trade-off is time: Friedrichstraße goes from a short hop to a real ride with a change, and bouldering evenings would mean crossing into Schöneberg or Kreuzberg. Weißensee stretches your budget furthest; Florakiez sits in between.
