If you move to Friedenau, the biggest change is the pace of your days. Kita drop-off becomes a walk under plane trees instead of along Torstraße, and Volkspark Wilmersdorf is close enough for after-school picnics. Rents are noticeably lower, which likely turns that third room from a stretch into a given.

The honest trade-off is time. Friedrichstraße goes from a short hop to a real ride with a change, and your bouldering evenings would mean crossing into Schöneberg or Kreuzberg. Weißensee stretches your budget furthest and puts a lake within a short walk — but three-room flats there are scarce. Florakiez sits in between: big parks and good Kitas, at roughly the rent you pay now.

How far is the nearest bouldering gym from Friedenau?

There isn’t one inside Friedenau itself. The closest halls are a short ride away in neighbouring districts — easy for a weekly session, harder for a spontaneous one once the kids are asleep.
